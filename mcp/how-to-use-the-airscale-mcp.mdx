## What is the Airscale MCP?

The Airscale MCP gives your assistant 22 typed tools that can:

- Check your workspace credit balance.
- Search for people and companies.
- Discover valid Find Companies filters before searching.
- Enrich contact details and person or company profiles.
- Run Airsearch for structured web research.
- Create downloadable CSV or JSONL exports.

See the [MCP tool catalog](/mcp/tools) for every tool and its inputs.

## How credits work

Find People costs 0.1 credit per returned person. Find Companies costs 0.1 credit per returned company. Airsearch costs 2 credits per call.

Credit checks, people counts, and Find Companies filter discovery are free planning tools. Other enrichment follows linked API pricing in the related references for [contact data](/api-reference/email-finder), [people profiles](/api-reference/extract-people-profile), and [company profiles](/api-reference/extract-company-profile).

Before a paid export starts, review the final filters, row limit, and maximum spend, then explicitly confirm that paid action. No paid search, enrichment, or export should run before your approval.

## Run a search in natural language

Describe the cohort and keep the first result small:

> Find up to five cybersecurity companies in France with 51–200 employees. Before you run it, show me the proposed tool, filters, row limit, and maximum cost, then wait for my approval.

Your assistant can use a free filter-discovery tool first when a value needs validation. Approve only the bounded search you reviewed.

### Review your results

Review the company name, domain, website, country, and filter precision before expanding the search. At 0.1 credit per returned company, a five-company sample costs at most 0.5 credits.

### Enrich contacts through conversation

Continue from only the companies you approved:

> For the approved companies, find and enrich at most one CEO, one CFO, and one Head of Growth per company. Show me the proposed paid action, row limit, and maximum cost, then wait for my approval.

Review the requested fields and linked pricing before approving enrichment. Keep each follow-up bounded, and do not add unapproved companies to the request.

## Export your final list

Exports are asynchronous: start one export, check its status, then retrieve the file after completion. Save the returned `export_id` and reuse the same `export_id` for status and file requests.

Follow the `poll_after_seconds` interval returned by each status response. Do not start a duplicate export while the existing job is queued or running.
