## 4. Access the results

**Deliverability classifications:**

- **Deliverable:** high confidence — safe for outreach campaigns
- **Risky (Catch-All):** use with caution — this domain accepts all emails, but the specific address may be invalid
- **Not Deliverable:** remove from campaigns to protect sender reputation
