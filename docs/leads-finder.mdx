**Note**

- You can import up to **10,000 people** per search
- Large searches may take several minutes to complete
- Use specific filters to get the most relevant results within the limit

## 0. Prerequisites

- An active Airschool account with remaining credits
- Clear understanding of your ideal customer profile (ICP)
- Specific criteria for your target prospects (people and company attributes)

## 1. Click on "Leads finder"

1. Log into your **Airschool** dashboard
2. Select **"Leads finder"** from the available modules (look for the Airschool logo icon)

**Note:** the "Leads finder" module is Airschool's native search engine, so you don't need any **external URLs or account connections**

![Leads finder — 1. Click on "Leads finder"](/images/docs/cqquFwDcJVTxgRdn407eEVXmiM.png)

## 2. Apply your search filters

Configure your search criteria using the available filters:

### People Filters

- Current company (you can select an Airschool table containing a list of companies, or use the manual input to enter them manually)
- Job titles
- Seniority level & Experience
- People Location
- Skills
- Language
- Certification
- Keywords in profile
- Profile Badge

### Company Filters

- Company industry
- Company size
- Company headquarter location
- Company type
- Annual revenue
- Keywords in profile
- Founded year range
- Funding
- Employee by department
- Headcount Growth

![Leads finder — Company Filters](/images/docs/6BLagRkGcqX8ioUrfO624P3K1w.png)

## 3. Visualize your results

**Click on "Preview search results"** to see a sample of your search results

1. **Review the preview data:**

   - Names and current job titles
   - Company names
   - Geographic locations
   - Linkedin URLs
2. **Check the results summary:**

   - Total number of matches (e.g., "25 of 558,008 total")
   - Quality of matches against your criteria
3. **Refine filters if needed:**

   - Add or remove people or company attributes based on preview results
   - Modify or refine location targeting for better coverage

**Note:** ensure the preview shows **relevant prospects before importing**. Poor preview results indicate filters need adjustment

![Leads finder — 3. Visualize your results](/images/docs/5EQyGnGy2IvWWQcju2S8W9fM2nQ.png)

## 4. Launch the search

1. **Click "Load all people to a table"** to export the full search to a table
2. **Monitor the import progress:**

   - Progress indicator shows "Importing: X/10 000"
   - Percentage completion display
   - Real-time status updates
3. **Wait for completion:**

   - You can navigate away during processing

**Note:** the system **automatically stops at 10,000 results** even if more matches exist. Use more specific filters if you hit this limit and want different prospects

![Leads finder — 4. Launch the search](/images/docs/dvVGNpKtk9dnxFi924XUaSptL2Q.png)

## 5. Access your results

**Click on "Access results"** to view your new prospect table:
