**Note:**

- You can import up to **1,000 companies** per search
- Large searches may take several minutes to complete
- Use specific filters to get the most relevant companies within the limit

## 0. Prerequisites

- An active Airscale account with remaining credits
- Clear understanding of your ideal customer profile (ICP)
- Specific criteria for your target companies (industry, size, location, etc.)

## 1. Click on "Find Companies (LinkedIn)"

- Log into your **Airscale** dashboard
- **Select "Find companies (LinkedIn)"** from the available modules (look for the LinkedIn logo)

![Find Companies (Linkedin) — 1. Click on "Find Companies (LinkedIn)"](/images/docs/jTFkcwzh6MLCjE5uHeLPcxgXz0.png)

## 2. Apply your search filters

Configure your search criteria using the comprehensive filter system:

### Basic company filters

**Location targeting:**

- **Include country, state, or city:** Enter geographic filters like "United States," "California," or "San Francisco"
- Multiple locations can be added for broader coverage
- Use specific cities for territory-based targeting

**Company industries:**

- Select from dropdown of industry categories
- Multiple industries can be selected
- Focus on industries where your solution has proven success

**Company size:**

- Choose from predefined ranges: 1-10, 11-50, 51-200, 201-500, 501-1000, 1001-5000, 5001-10000
- Select multiple ranges for broader targeting
- Consider your product's sweet spot for company size

### Advanced growth and financial filters

**Company size growth:**

- Set minimum and maximum growth percentages
- Target companies experiencing specific growth rates
- Filter for rapidly expanding businesses or stable enterprises

**Company signals:**

- Select from dropdown of buying intent and behavioral signals
- Target companies showing signs of growth, hiring, or technology adoption
- Focus on businesses likely to be in market for your solution

**Company revenue:**

- Set minimum and maximum revenue in millions
- Target companies within specific revenue ranges
- Align with your solution's typical customer spend capacity

### Department-specific filters

**Company department count:**

- Select specific departments (Sales, Marketing, Engineering, etc.)
- Set minimum and maximum employee counts within those departments
- Target companies with mature departments in your focus area

**Company department growth:**

- Choose specific departments experiencing growth
- Set minimum and maximum growth percentages for those departments
- Identify companies actively hiring in relevant areas

**Company followers count:**

- Select a specific range of follower count on LinkedIn
- Target companies with strong brand presence or niche audiences
- Filter by growth stage based on community size

### Advanced keyword targeting

**Keywords filter:** Use sophisticated keyword combinations to find companies with specific business models or strategies.

**Business model examples:**

- `freemium AND self-serve AND PLG` (Product-Led Growth companies)
- `subscription AND SaaS AND upselling` (SaaS businesses)
- `shopify AND DTC AND ecommerce` (Direct-to-consumer brands)

**Go-to-Market strategy examples:**

- `(cold email OR cold call) AND B2B AND outbound` (Outbound sales focus)
- `(ABM OR demand gen) AND enterprise` (Account-based marketing)

**Technology focus examples:**

- `AI AND chatbot AND customer support` (AI customer service)
- `fintech AND KYC AND compliance` (Financial technology)
- `logistics AND warehouse AND last mile` (Supply chain/logistics)

**Sustainability examples:**

- `sustainability AND green energy AND carbon` (Green technology)

**Pro Tip:** you don't need to press enter when typing keyword combinations. Just type the keywords and Boolean operators directly (AND/OR)

## 3. Visualize your results

**Click "PREVIEW COMPANIES"** to see a sample of your search results.

1. **Review the preview data:**

   - Company names: check if businesses match your target market
   - Industry classifications: verify industry relevance to your solution
   - Company descriptions: review business models and focus areas
   - LinkedIn profile URLs: validate company authenticity and presence
2. **Check the results summary:**

   - Total number of matches
   - Quality of matches against your ICP
   - Distribution across industries and business models
3. **Refine filters if needed:**

   - Adjust keyword combinations based on company descriptions
   - Modify industry selections for better targeting
   - Update size or revenue ranges based on company profiles
   - Fine-tune growth criteria for ideal timing

**Note:** ensure the preview shows **relevant companies before importing**. Poor preview results indicate filters need adjustment

![Find Companies (Linkedin) — 3. Visualize your results](/images/docs/qVDao0MlaUzH9rCFNt0AcJ5Tg.png)

## 4. Click on "Load all companies to a table" to create a new table

**Review your final filter settings** one more time

1. **Click "Load all companies to a table"** to export the full search
2. **Monitor the import progress:**

   - Progress indicator shows "Importing: X/1000"
   - Percentage completion display
   - Real-time status updates
3. **Wait for completion:**

   - You can navigate away during processing

**Note:** the system automatically stops at **1,000 results** even if more matches exist. Use more specific filters if you hit this limit and want different prospects

![Find Companies (Linkedin) — 4. Click on "Load all companies to a table" to create a new table](/images/docs/MCxC1XuMQudqQnZBsC4ugBzefZM.png)

## 5. See your results

**Click on "Access results"** to view your new company table:
