**Note:** you can only import up to **1,000 companies** per search

## 1. Click on "Find companies hiring for a specific position"

![Find companies hiring for specific positions — 1. Click on "Find companies hiring for a specific position"](/images/docs/eIc2Yoaw65kiCyN9tGRLZtKQ1B8.png)

## 2. Add your search filters

Use the following filters to refine your search:

- **Query:** "Head of Growth", "Data Analyst", etc.
- **Company Headcount:** filter by size to match your ICP (e.g., 11–50, 51–200)
- **Industry:** target relevant industries (e.g., Health, Consumer Services, etc.)
- **Location:** focus on specific countries, regions, or cities
- etc.

![Find companies hiring for specific positions — 2. Add your search filters](/images/docs/ETPjevwkEdqLCKjLxexTkECaY.png)

## 3. Preview your search results

![Find companies hiring for specific positions — 3. Preview your search results](/images/docs/QTeHpVYsA9ZQBYuFasgdFX27cyE.png)

## 4. Launch the extraction and access your results

Click on **"Load companies to a table"** to begin the scraping process and load the companies to an Airscale table

![Find companies hiring for specific positions — 4. Launch the extraction and access your results](/images/docs/RS1tl0ZjLBcysSlaNTBbqaSoU.png)
