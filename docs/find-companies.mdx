**Note:**

- You can import up to **10,000 companies** per search
- Large searches may take several minutes to complete
- Use specific filters to get the most relevant companies within the limit

## 0. Prerequisites

- An active Airschool account with remaining credits
- Clear understanding of your ideal customer profile (ICP)
- Specific criteria for your target companies (industry, size, location, etc.)

## 1. Click on "Find Companies"

- Log into your **Airschool** dashboard
- **Select "Find companies"** from the available modules (look for the Airschool icon)

**Note:** the Find Companies module is Airschool's native search engine, so you don't need any external URLs or account connections

![Find Companies — 1. Click on "Find Companies"](/images/docs/JPMGGavgTaLa4qXjv6IQT7RTIg.png)

## 2. Apply your search filters

Configure your search criteria using the comprehensive filter system:

### Basic company filters

**Company industry:**

- Select from dropdown of industry categories
- Multiple industries can be selected
- Focus on industries where your solution has proven success

**Company size:**

- Choose from predefined ranges: 1-10, 11-50, 51-200, 201-500, 501-1000, 1001-5000, 5001-10000
- Select multiple ranges for broader targeting
- Consider your product's sweet spot for company size

**Headquarter location targeting:**

- Include country, state, or city: Enter geographic filters like "United States," "California," or "San Francisco"
- Multiple locations can be added for broader coverage
- Use specific cities for territory-based targeting

**Company number of locations & company age:**

- Set minimum and maximum number of office locations as well as company age in years
- Target single-site startups or multi-location enterprises depending on your ideal customer profile
- Align outreach with companies at the right maturity and operational complexity for your solution

![Find Companies — Basic company filters](/images/docs/GNzzXexOisk69kbX1VHyoNN3M.png)

### Advanced growth filters & signals

**Company recent events:**

- Select multiple recent events simultaneously:

  - *Hiring in a specific department*
  - *Increase in a specific department*
  - *Decrease in a specific department*
  - *Company award*
  - *New product*
  - *Employee joined company*
  - *Merger and acquisitions*
  - *Lawsuit and legal issues*
  - *Outages and security breaches*
  - *Closing office*
  - *New investment*
  - *New office*
  - *New partnership*
  - *Cost cutting*
  - *New funding round*
  - *Award*
  - *IPO announcement*
- Identify companies going through strategic shifts or growth phases
- Prioritize outreach based on timely, contextual triggers
- Filter by last occurrence : 30, 60, or 90 days

**Company revenue:**

- Set minimum and maximum revenue in millions
- Target companies within specific revenue ranges
- Align with your solution's typical customer spend capacity

**Topics recently searched:**

- Select specific topics or keywords recently searched by target companies
- Identify businesses actively researching solutions in your space
- Prioritize outreach based on real-time intent signals to reach prospects at the right moment in their buying journey

**Company tech stack:**

- Select specific technologies used by target companies (CRM, marketing tools, sales tools, infrastructure, etc.)
- Filter companies based on their current tech stack to identify integration opportunities or competitive displacement
- Target businesses already familiar with similar solutions to reduce onboarding friction

![Find Companies — Advanced growth filters & signals](/images/docs/3CF93jgX4Q9NQkYoPc3XqnIFck.png)

### Advanced keyword targeting

**Keywords in website:**

- Enter specific keywords to target companies whose website content matches your criteria
- Identify businesses operating in niche industries or verticals based on their own messaging
- Reach companies that explicitly address topics relevant to your solution on their public pages

## 3. Visualize your results

**Click "PREVIEW COMPANIES"** to see a sample of your search results.

1. **Review the preview data:**

   - Company names
   - Company website
   - Company location
2. **Check the results summary:**

   - Total number of matches
   - Quality of matches against your ICP
   - Distribution across industries and business models
3. **Refine filters if needed:**

   - Adjust keyword combinations based on company descriptions
   - Modify industry selections for better targeting
   - Update size or revenue ranges based on company profiles
   - Fine-tune company events for ideal timing

**Note:** ensure the preview shows **relevant companies before importing**. Poor preview results indicate filters need adjustment

![Find Companies — 3. Visualize your results](/images/docs/EfJJiiHwZKdE9Zqxstah0yrjJg.png)

## 4. Click on "Load all companies to a table" to create a new table

**Review your final filter settings** one more time

1. **Click "Load all companies to a table"** to export the full search
2. **Monitor the import progress:**

   - Progress indicator shows "Importing: X/10,000"
   - Percentage completion display
   - Real-time status updates
3. **Wait for completion:**

   - You can navigate away during processing

**Note:** the system automatically stops at **10,000 results** even if more matches exist. Use more specific filters if you hit this limit and want different prospects

![Find Companies — 4. Click on "Load all companies to a table" to create a new table](/images/docs/JXEnxUGXAX2po1p59KVF32c.png)

## 5. See your results

**Click on "Access results"** to view your new company table:

![Find Companies — 5. See your results](/images/docs/tNlmlneySCEqUJ8k9GDqtSoq7Yg.png)

You can now start enriching!
