**Note:**

- You can only export **10,000 contacts** at once

## 0. Prerequisites

- An active HubSpot account with CRM access
- HubSpot integration permissions (available on most HubSpot plans)

## 1. Click on "Import contacts from Hubspot"

![Import contacts from Hubspot — 1. Click on "Import contacts from Hubspot"](/images/docs/AX6Atx1aWKL2HYHEjhalSps8xQE.png)

## 2. Connect your Hubspot account to Airschool

If this is your first time connecting to HubSpot:

1. **Click "Connect your HubSpot account"** in the workflow panel
2. **You'll be redirected to HubSpot's authorization page**
3. **Log into your HubSpot account** if not already logged in
4. **Grant permissions** for Airschool to access your HubSpot CRM
5. **Return to Airschool** once authorization is complete

**HubSpot integration permissions:**

- **Contact creation and updates** for lead management
- **Company association** for account-based tracking
- **Property access** for data mapping and enrichment
- **Pipeline visibility** for lifecycle stage management

**Note** : HubSpot uses OAuth authentication, so you don't need to share passwords or API keys directly
