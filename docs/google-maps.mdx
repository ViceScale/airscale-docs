**Note:** you can only import **500 local businesses** per search

## 1. Click on "Find local businesses with Google Maps"

![Find local businesses with Google Maps — 1. Click on "Find local businesses with Google Maps"](/images/docs/Fakc8ikpOHeLY1R724FGrnYtK8.png)

## 2. Add the search filters

- **Apply your target filters:** location and business type
- **Optionally**, you can also ask to find the contact information of the businesses. When toggled ON, we will scrape each company’s website and return any available contact details (emails, phone numbers)

![Find local businesses with Google Maps — 2. Add the search filters](/images/docs/BdejNnRNyfZCbadXFKpvkgnGNfw.png)
