**Note:** you can extract up to **3000 post likers/commenters** at once

## 0. Prerequisites

- Access to LinkedIn (personal or Sales Navigator account)
- Specific LinkedIn posts with engagement (likes/comments) to target

## 1. Click on "Extract LinkedIn post likers and commenters"

1. Log into your **Airschool account**
2. Select **"Extract LinkedIn post likers and commenters"** from the available options in the **"Find People"** tab

![Extract post likers/commenters — 1. Click on "Extract LinkedIn post likers and commenters"](/images/docs/mTeyja6PKB6NJK40FPIlseAoGsE.png)

## 2. Copy the URL of the post

Identify and prepare your target LinkedIn post:

1. **Find a relevant LinkedIn post** with good engagement:

   - Industry thought leadership content
   - Company announcements from competitors
   - Product launches or feature updates
   - Industry news or trend discussions
2. **Copy the post URL:**

   - Navigate to the specific LinkedIn post
   - Click on the post timestamp to open the individual post view
   - Copy the full URL from your browser's address bar
   - URL should look like: `https://www.linkedin.com/posts/[username]_[post-id]`
3. **Give your extraction a descriptive name:**

   - Example: "SaaS CEO Post Engagement | Q1 2024 | Marketing Automation"
   - Include the post topic and date for easy reference
4. **Paste the post URL** into the designated field in Airschool

**Pro tip:** choose posts with **50+ engagements** for a meaningful prospect list, but avoid **super viral posts** (3000+ engagements) that may contain less targeted audiences

![Extract post likers/commenters — 2. Copy the URL of the post](/images/docs/4PVO3KHqPr9ebCdzQMohTCvUQ.png)

![Extract post likers/commenters — 2. Copy the URL of the post](/images/docs/ka9IHgnXGXmlJZYm33USvTWtAg.png)

## 3. Launch the extraction

**Click "LAUNCH EXTRACTION"** to begin the scraping process

1. **Monitor progress in real-time:**

   - Validating post access and engagement data
   - Extracting user profiles from engagement
   - Processing and importing results
2. **Wait for completion** — most extractions complete within 1–5 minutes

**Pro tip:** you can navigate away from this page during extraction

![Extract post likers/commenters — 3. Launch the extraction](/images/docs/66wC8gsh7AEcMAoLPDzP21Yfc.png)
