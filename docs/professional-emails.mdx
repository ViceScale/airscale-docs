- First name
- Last name
- Company name **or** company domain

**OR**

- People LinkedIn URL

## 4. Access the results

Once the enrichment is complete, you can see which provider returned the verified email by clicking on **"Details"**

**Note:**

**Email status indicators:**

- **Deliverable:** email exists and is safe to contact
- **Not found:** no email address could be found
- **Risky:** email found but deliverability uncertain (often catch-all domains)
- **Found:** email address discovered and verified

**Deliverability classifications:**

- **Deliverable:** high confidence — safe for outreach campaigns
- **Risky (Catch-All):** use with caution: this domain accepts all emails, but the specific address may be invalid
- **Not Deliverable:** remove from campaigns to protect sender reputation
