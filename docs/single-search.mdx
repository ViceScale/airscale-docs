## 1. Click on "Single Search"

![Single Search — 1. Click on "Single Search"](/images/docs/t4wm0y2IGeG2OxYVw1J3zWWxAlE.png)

## 2. Copy the profile URL of a profile and paste it below

![Single Search — 2. Copy the profile URL of a profile and paste it below](/images/docs/03S62s6Q6XiRIbWcWuauIcUEg8U.png)

## 3. Specify whether you want to find their professional email and phone numbers

**Note:** you can toggle ON/OFF options to find emails and phone numbers

![Single Search — 3. Specify whether you want to find their professional email and phone numbers](/images/docs/wzJsa14q3rdIXjP9TkXGAXTh5sE.png)

## 4. Launch the extraction and export the profile to a table

- **Click "EXTRACT PROFILE"** to begin the scraping process
- After a few seconds, we will retrieve the data about the profile, and the results will appear on the right side of the screen

![Single Search — 4. Launch the extraction and export the profile to a table](/images/docs/w88qp3aqGy6JaQSzGyUr8dzniSY.png)
