---

## 1. Click on the settings of your column

![Transfer column content — 1. Click on the settings of your column](/images/docs/OYPnHJsOjKzR6J5pTe2wLCtGOcY.png)

## 2. Click on "Transfer data to another column"

![Transfer column content — 2. Click on "Transfer data to another column"](/images/docs/IK5WDeFbLgqANMudAsuR6Doyj2E.png)

## 3. Select the column to which you want to transfer the data

![Transfer column content — 3. Select the column to which you want to transfer the data](/images/docs/4RIlmBDekFQL8eDywbIu58oCdE.png)

## 4. Choose the number of rows you want to transfer

![Transfer column content — 4. Choose the number of rows you want to transfer](/images/docs/4i5kLpAMYtvK4i5nCyuSAxZOFY.png)

**Note:** clicking "Transfer Data" copies the data to the new column without removing it from the original one
