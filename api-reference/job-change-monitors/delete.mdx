---
title: "Delete a job-change monitor"
description: "Permanently remove a monitor and its stored monitoring data."
canonical: "https://airscale.mintlify.app/api-reference/job-change-monitors/delete"
openapi: "/openapi.json DELETE /v1/job-change-monitors/{monitor_id}"
---

Delete a monitor by `monitor_id`. This permanently removes the monitor, its profile baselines, and its events from the workspace.

<Warning>
Deletion cannot be undone. Remove individual profiles instead when you need to stop checking a contact while retaining its event history.
</Warning>

The operation returns `{ "ok": true }` after the monitor and its cascaded data are deleted.

## Next step

Use [List job-change monitors](/api-reference/job-change-monitors/list) to verify the monitor no longer appears.
