description: "Find a professional email address for one person."

---

Identify one person by LinkedIn profile URL or by name and company, then return a verified professional email when one is found.

<Note>
The limit is 3,000 requests per minute per workspace. A successful result costs 2 credits; `not_found` is not charged.
</Note>

At least one complete identification form is required. You may send both forms when you have them.

<Warning>
Use bounded backoff after a transient `502` or `503`. A `503` can mean a successful result could not be settled by the credit service; retry the same input only after the service recovers.
</Warning>
