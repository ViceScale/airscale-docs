In this video, we walk through a practical Airschool workflow: scraping local businesses from Google Maps, identifying the right decision-makers inside those companies, and enriching their professional contact data.

## Scrape local businesses with Google Maps

The process starts with the **Find Local Businesses with Google Maps** module, accessible from the **Find Companies** section.

Configure your search by entering:

- A location (e.g. New York)
- A query (e.g. real estate brokers)
- An optional result limit (up to 500 companies per table)

You can also choose to include contact information, which will trigger additional scraping from each company's website.

Once launched, Airschool builds a table automatically populated with key fields, including:

- Company name
- **Company domain**
- Company location
- Phone number
- Business type

## Find decision-makers in those companies

From the table, click **Enrich** and navigate to the **Find People** category, then select **Find People in Companies**.

Configure the enrichment with:

- The company domain as input
- The job titles you want to find (e.g. Owner, CEO)
- A people location filter

> **Important:** this enrichment returns one contact per company, prioritizing the first job title listed. If you need multiple decision-makers per company, see the alternative method below.

Once complete, Airschool automatically adds several new columns: first name, last name, job title, and LinkedIn URL.

## Enrich verified professional emails

With the LinkedIn URL or company domain available, you can run an email enrichment directly on the results.

Emails found are automatically verified by Airschool: no additional verification tool needed, and no credits are spent when no data is found.

## Finding multiple job titles per company

If you need to find several roles within the same companies, use the **Leads Finder** module instead:

1. Go to the home section and open Leads Finder
2. Under People attributes → Current company, select **from an Airschool table** and choose your existing company table
3. Use the company domain as the matching field
4. Add as many job titles as needed
5. Preview and load the results

This approach allows you to retrieve multiple decision-makers per company in a single search.
